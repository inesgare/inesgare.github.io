# Topological and Geometric Deep Learning: Theory, Methods, and Applications

<p class="course-page-meta">Doctoral course &nbsp;·&nbsp; Universidad Complutense de Madrid &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026 </p>

This is a page dedicated to the course on Topological and Geometric Deep Learning that I gave in May, 2026 at the Universidad Complutense of Madrid. This intends to be an introduction to the use of topological and geometric tools in deep learning, to learn from topological and combinatorial domains such as graphs or simplicial complexes, but also to better understand how these systems learn.

This is a non-exhaustive course, focused on persistent homology as the main tool, but there are many other concepts that could be used for the same purpose (Euler transforms, curvature, magnitude) that are not mentioned in the course. I have tried to add the most relevant references for each topic to the slides, but of course, I might have missed something! Feel free to reach out if I have neglected something important in this regard.
